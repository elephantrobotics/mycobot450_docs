# Calibration

In the Program interface, select the Calibration function by clicking the asterisk (*), and press the C key to enter the Calibration function.

<img src="../../../resources/3-FunctionsAndApplications/5-BasicApplication/5.4/5.4.5-calibration/5.4.5-calibration_show.png" width="300" align="center"/>

Once in the Calibration function, select the joint you want to calibrate. Then, use the A and B keys to rotate the selected joint. A short press moves the step angle by 0.1°, and a long press is supported. After moving to the desired zero position, press the C key to save. At this point, the corresponding joint angle data on the screen will immediately change to 0°.
